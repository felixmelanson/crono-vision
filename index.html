Tip

Show your hand in the photo as a fiducial scale reference. Plate and bowl sizes vary a lot; hand size doesn't — it pins down portion estimates far more tightly than tableware does.

Data & storage

Nothing is stored by this app. Each photo is analyzed and discarded — it never touches your camera roll or sits on a server disk. Diary entries are written straight to your own Cronometer account.
